/ About Me

Seeking the truth through fearless Writing.

Professional journalist with experience in television news, digital media, and multilingual content production. Skilled in news writing, editing, translation (English–Tamil), script preparation, and news bulletin handling. Passionate about delivering accurate, impactful, and audience-focused stories with a strong interest in public affairs, social issues, and development journalism. Committed to ethical reporting, clear communication, and creating meaningful content across broadcast and digital platforms.

My career philosophy is simple: remain independent, ask the hard questions, and never back down from a complex story. I strive to bring clarity to chaos and context to the headlines.
