/ About Me

Seeking the truth through fearless storytelling.

I am a professional journalist with over a decade of experience in uncovering stories that matter. My work focuses on the intersection of politics, social justice, and systemic inequality. I believe in the power of words to hold power accountable and to amplify the voices of the marginalized.

My career philosophy is simple: remain independent, ask the hard questions, and never back down from a complex story. I strive to bring clarity to chaos and context to the headlines.
